About Izuire

IZUIRE is the bridge between Chinese manufacturers and African buyers, built to make sourcing simpler, safer and more transparent.

Our story

Built from the trade gap we saw firsthand

Izuire Co. Ltd. was founded to close a gap that shows up again and again for African importers: finding reliable Chinese suppliers, verifying quality before money changes hands, and getting goods home without losing money in the process.

Operating out of Guangzhou with strong roots in Onitsha, Nigeria, Izuire sits physically where the sourcing happens, inside the markets and factory networks of one of the world's largest manufacturing hubs, while staying close to the realities of the African businesses it serves.

Izuire Co. Ltd. is registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC), with its scope covering importation, exportation, and general trading of consumer goods and merchandise.

Mission

To give African buyers direct, trustworthy access to Chinese manufacturers, with sourcing, quality control and logistics handled end-to-end.

Vision

To be the most trusted bridge for China-to-Africa trade, known for transparency where the industry has historically had none.

What makes us different

A team physically based in Guangzhou, direct supplier relationships built over years in the market, and a straightforward process, no middlemen stacking markups you can't see.
