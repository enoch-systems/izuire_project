What's included

Our sourcing services

Use one service or all eleven, most orders touch most of these steps.

Product Sourcing

Tell us what you need, a photo, a link, or a description, and we track it down across Guangzhou's markets and factories.

Manufacturer Sourcing

For larger or custom orders, we go straight to the factory floor, connecting you with manufacturers, not just middlemen.

Supplier Verification

Every supplier we work with is checked for legitimacy, capacity and track record before your order goes anywhere near them.

Price Negotiation

We negotiate on your behalf using market knowledge you likely don't have access to on your own, and pass the savings to you.

MOQ Assistance

Minimum order quantities can make or break a first order. We negotiate lower MOQs where possible, or help you consolidate to meet them.

Quality Control

Standards are agreed upfront, and production is checked against them, not left to chance until the goods arrive at your door.

Product Inspection

Every order is physically inspected before it ships, with photo and video proof sent to you for sign-off.

White Labeling

Put your own brand on eligible products, from private-label packaging to fully custom manufacturing runs.

Packaging & Custom Branding

Custom boxes, labels, and inserts, designed and produced to match how you want to present the product to your customers.

Order Monitoring

You get visibility into where your order stands, from production through inspection, instead of radio silence until it ships.

Shipping Coordination

We handle freight booking, documentation and customs coordination so your order moves without you chasing three different parties.

How a sourcing order moves

1

You tell us what you need

Product, quantity, spec, target price, as much or as little detail as you have.

2

We source & quote

We pull options from vetted suppliers and come back with pricing and MOQ.

3

You approve

Confirm the quote and terms before anything moves into production or purchase.

4

We inspect & ship

Quality checked, then consolidated and shipped to your destination.
