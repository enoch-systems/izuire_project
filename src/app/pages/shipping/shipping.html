Understanding CBM

What is CBM, and why does it matter?

CBM (Cubic Meter) measures the volume your shipment takes up, length × width × height, in meters. For sea freight especially, CBM (not just weight) is often what determines your shipping cost, since cargo space is sold by volume. Knowing your CBM upfront helps us quote accurately and helps you compare air vs. sea for your specific order.
