Understanding CBM

What is CBM, and why does it matter for your quote?

CBM (Cubic Meter) measures the volume your shipment takes up, length × width × height in meters. For sea freight especially, CBM (not just weight) is what determines your shipping cost, since container space is sold by volume. Estimate yours below.
